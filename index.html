Calculation methodology & units
Assumptions, limits & OEE

Station capacities are effective capacities, not rated capacities. The serial line throughput is limited by its slowest station. Production equals sales; there is no inventory accumulation. Annual operating hours and average power are fixed independently of demand, including at zero sales. Conveyor animation is a qualitative representation of steady-state throughput, not a validated discrete-event simulation.

Cash flows are constant, pre-tax, unlevered and incremental to the same-assumption baseline. No terminal value, tax, depreciation tax shield, working capital, financing costs, ramp-up, downtime or uncertainty distribution is modelled. Unused capacity is not directly valued as financial waste. An optional user-supplied emissions factor is unverified.

OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality. OEE is explanatory only in version 2; it is not multiplied into the already-effective capacities.

Tied bottlenecks must be improved together to unlock the next capacity level. Improving just one tied station produces no immediate line gain.
